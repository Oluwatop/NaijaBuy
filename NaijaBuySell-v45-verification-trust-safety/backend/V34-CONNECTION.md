# V34 Connection
Connects the marketplace to Supabase services: authentication, profiles, listing browsing/creation, orders and buyer receipt confirmation.
Create `.env` with `VITE_SUPABASE_URL` and `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`.
Do not expose a service-role key. Real payment collection/payout remains disabled until an appropriate compliant protected-payment arrangement is selected.
