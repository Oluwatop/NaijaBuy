# V37 Real Estate + Seller Verification

NaijaBuy&Sell now supports:
- Houses
- Apartments/flats
- Land
- Shops
- Offices/commercial property
- Warehouses
- Estates/developments
- Short-let properties
- Sale, rent and lease listing purposes
- Property-specific fields such as bedrooms, bathrooms, land size, address and furnished status
- Seller verification status fields
- Seller public profile

Important marketplace rule:
Real-estate listings should be subject to moderation and seller verification. Do not advertise ownership, title, approvals, survey plans or other legal facts unless the seller provides evidence and the platform's verification process has actually checked them.

Next production work:
- Property listing form with conditional real-estate fields
- Property photo gallery
- Map/location support
- Document verification workflow
- Agent/developer profiles
- Property-specific search filters
- Admin verification queue and audit log
