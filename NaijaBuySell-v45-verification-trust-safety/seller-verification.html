Seller & Property Verification

Verification should be performed by authorized administrators using evidence appropriate to the seller and property type.

Important: Do not display a “verified” badge merely because a seller self-declared ownership or agency status. Sensitive identity/property documents should be handled through a secure verification process, not exposed in public listings.

Recommended review

Seller

  • Account and contact verification
  • Seller/agent identity review
  • Business/agency details where applicable
  • Complaint and moderation history

Property

  • Ownership/authority evidence where appropriate
  • Property description consistency
  • Location and listing details
  • Duplicate or suspicious listing checks
